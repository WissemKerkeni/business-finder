### What the Google reviews show (54 reviews)
The large majority of reviews (about 35 of 54) are about **transfers and day excursions with a driver**, not self-drive rental:
- Airport transfers: Monastir airport → Mahdia hotel (H B, Susana Campelo, DAHROUCH CIRINE), Sfax → Mahdia (marie-aude martin), about 1 h trip
- Day trips: El Jem (amphitheatre and museum), Sousse, Kairouan, Sidi Bou Said, Tunis, Hammamet, Monastir
- Vehicles mentioned: "simples berlines … jusqu'au van 9 places" (martial remacle); "9-passenger vehicle" (Марія Дорош); "clean, well maintained, with AC" (Filip Radojicic)
- Drivers named in reviews: Gaïth, Sadok, Hachem, Hedi, Said, Medì; Hamdi is described as "le gestionnaire"
- Languages: one review says the guide "speaks very good Italian"; another reviewer (English speaker) says the driver helped with translation
- Self-drive rental is mentioned by: ZRELLI SLIM ("Les voitures sont neuves, propres…"), Mohammed Alamgir ("car hire"), Rayen Jemmali ("clean cars, good service, reasonable prices")

## Visual identity
- **Logo:** a red outline car swoosh above "TOP CAR" in bold italic red, with "LOCATION de VOITURE" underneath, on a charcoal square (#2B2B2E approx). It also appears in white on dark.
- **Post style (2024–25):** a red horizontal band with the white slogan "Roulez En Toute Sécurité", a charcoal logo tile, and a contact strip at the bottom (icons + 50 202 203 · 73 653 271 · MAHDIA · email). Deep navy and charcoal backgrounds, with red as the single accent.
- **Earlier posts (2023):** "TOP CAR CONFORT" in grey stencil on photo backgrounds, and yellow/black "TOP CAR CONFORT".
- **Palette to derive:** charcoal/near-black (#1E1F22), signal red (#E3202B approx, from the band and logo), white, and cool grey (#9AA0A6). Navy (#12173A) is a secondary tone taken from posts.
- **Language:** posts are in **French**; greetings are in Arabic (Aïd, Ramadan) and some in English. The clientele in the reviews is international (EN, FR, IT, DE, PT, UK/RU). → Site in **French first**. An English version is an option to raise with the owner.

## Delivery / pickup locations
- The agency offers **airport transfers** (a service with a driver): "Transport aéroport" (FB flyer). There is a real photo at **Aéroport Monastir Habib Bourguiba** (fb_35), and reviews mention Monastir airport and Sfax.
- Hotel pickup/drop-off for transfers: reviews mention "transferts domicile / hôtel Mahdia". FB flyers show the vans at a Royal El Mansour / Iberostar hotel entrance (fb_11), but no partnership is stated, so don't claim one.
- **No self-drive car delivery** is stated. → The request bar uses a free-text "Lieu de prise en charge" plus suggestions limited to "Agence (Av. Taher Sfar)" and "Aéroport Monastir (transfert)". The owner needs to confirm this.

## Conflicts and stale items for the owner
1. **Landline 73 653 271** appears only on FB. Should it go on the site?
2. **Instagram:** the flyers show an Instagram icon ("TOPCAR"), but no account was found. What is the handle?
3. **Fleet:** please confirm the current models (Picanto, Fiat 500, Rio, MG 5, MG ZS, Peugeot van, utility vans), with transmission, fuel and seats.
4. **Prices:** none published. Should the site stay on "Prix sur demande", or do they want to publish from-prices (per day/week/month)?
5. **Rental conditions** (deposit, age, licence, mileage, insurance): none published, so the section is omitted.
6. **Transfers at night:** Google hours are Mon–Sat 9–19, but reviews mention transfers at 2:45 and 6:00. Are transfers available 24/7 or on request? The site won't claim either until they confirm.
7. **Airports served for transfers:** Monastir (photo + reviews) and Sfax (review). Is Enfidha served too?
8. **Negative reviews:** 3 × 1★ (two night transfers where no one came, 2025; one where the office and phone went unanswered). There are no owner replies.
9. **Possible self-reviews** by staff accounts (hedi halaoui, hachem naceur, Msakni Hamdi).
10. **Real car photos:** the site would be much stronger with 5–10 plain photos of their own cars (front 3/4, no text). Ask the owner for originals.
