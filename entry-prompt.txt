# Business Lead Research — Monastir → Mahdia → Sousse

Act as a **local business lead researcher**. Your goal is to find businesses in Tunisia that could benefit from having a professional **brochure/informational website**.

## Target regions — strict order

Search in this exact order:

1. **Monastir Governorate**
2. **Mahdia Governorate**
3. **Sousse Governorate**

Do not mix the regions. Complete the Monastir search first, then Mahdia, then Sousse.

Prioritize cities and towns within each governorate, including smaller local areas rather than only the main city.

## Where to search

Use multiple sources whenever possible:

* Google Maps / Google Business Profiles
* Yawakit — https://yawakit.com/
* Google Search
* Business directories
* Facebook business pages
* Instagram business pages
* Local business directories
* Other publicly available websites/directories

Use Google Maps as the primary source for discovering businesses, then use other sources to verify their contact information and online presence.

## Target businesses

Look for local businesses such as:

* Restaurants
* Cafés
* Bakeries / pastry shops
* Hotels / guest houses
* Car rental companies
* Car dealerships
* Auto repair shops
* Car accessory stores
* Beauty salons / barbers
* Gyms / fitness centers
* Furniture stores
* Clothing stores
* Electronics stores
* Phone / computer shops
* Construction companies
* Plumbing / electrical companies
* Real-estate agencies
* Travel agencies
* Medical/dental clinics
* Veterinary clinics
* Opticians
* Wedding/event businesses
* Photography studios
* Cleaning companies
* Printing businesses
* Industrial/service companies
* Other local businesses where a brochure website could provide value

Do NOT limit the research to these categories. If you find another type of business that clearly could benefit from a professional website, include it.

## Main qualification criteria

I am specifically looking for businesses that **do NOT appear to have a proper brochure/informational website**.

A business qualifies when:

* It has no website listed on Google Maps, OR
* The listed website is missing/broken, OR
* The business only has Facebook/Instagram/social media and no dedicated website, OR
* The website is clearly not a proper business website, OR
* The website is only a marketplace/directory/social-media profile rather than the company's own website.

### Important distinction

Do NOT automatically classify a business as having "no website" just because Google Maps doesn't show one.

Search the business name + city on Google and check other public sources.

Also distinguish between:

* **No website**
* **Social media only**
* **Website exists**
* **Unclear / needs manual verification**

Only include businesses as strong leads when there is reasonable evidence that they don't have a proper brochure website.

## Information to collect

For every qualified business, collect:

1. Business name
2. Business category/type
3. City
4. Governorate
5. Full address
6. Phone number
7. Secondary phone number if available
8. Google Maps link
9. Facebook page, if available
10. Instagram page, if available
11. Website, if any
12. Website status
13. Short reason why it is a potential website lead
14. Source(s) used to verify the information

## Phone numbers

Phone numbers are very important.

Try to find and verify the business phone number from:

* Google Maps
* Official Facebook page
* Official Instagram page
* Business directories
* Other reliable public sources

Prefer the most recently listed business number.

Do not invent or guess phone numbers.

If multiple numbers exist, include them and indicate which appears to be the primary number.

## Website verification

For each business, actively check whether a website exists.

Search combinations such as:

`"Business Name" + city`

`"Business Name" + Tunisia`

`"Business Name" + website`

Check Google results and the business's social media profiles.

Classify website status as exactly one of:

* `No website found`
* `Social media only`
* `Website exists`
* `Website unclear`

For my lead list, prioritize:

**No website found** and **Social media only**.

## Lead quality

Add a `Lead Potential` field:

* **High** — established business, no proper website, clear public phone number
* **Medium** — likely suitable but website status or business information requires some verification
* **Low** — weak evidence or questionable opportunity

Do not include businesses with an obvious modern, functioning brochure/business website unless there is another strong reason they could be a lead.

## Avoid duplicates

Deduplicate businesses carefully.

The same business may appear on:

* Google Maps
* Facebook
* Instagram
* Yellow Pages/directories
* Multiple Google results

Treat them as one business.

## Search strategy

Be systematic rather than stopping after finding a few businesses.

For each city/town:

1. Search Google Maps for different business categories.
2. Record promising businesses.
3. Search each promising business on Google.
4. Verify whether it has a proper website.
5. Find and verify its phone number.
6. Check Facebook/Instagram when useful.
7. Remove duplicates.
8. Move to the next category.
9. Continue until the area has been reasonably covered.

Use multiple search queries for each category because Google Maps results can vary depending on the query.

## Output

Create a structured table with these columns:

| # | Business | Type | City | Governorate | Phone | Website Status | Website | Google Maps | Facebook | Instagram | Lead Potential | Reason |
| - | -------- | ---- | ---- | ----------- | ----- | -------------- | ------- | ----------- | -------- | --------- | -------------- | ------ |

Keep **Monastir, Mahdia, and Sousse clearly separated**.

Within each governorate, group businesses by city.

## Priority

The final results should be ordered:

**1. Monastir → 2. Mahdia → 3. Sousse**

Within each region, prioritize:

**High potential → Medium potential → Low potential**

Do not give me businesses that already have a good professional brochure website just to increase the number of results.

## Final summary

At the end provide:

* Total qualified businesses
* Number in Monastir
* Number in Mahdia
* Number in Sousse
* Number with no website
* Number with social media only
* Number requiring manual verification
* Breakdown by business type

Also provide a separate **Top Leads** section containing the strongest opportunities, with their business name, type, city, phone number, and reason they are a good website prospect.

### Important

Accuracy is more important than quantity.

Do not invent missing information.

Clearly mark information that could not be verified.

Use publicly available business information only.
