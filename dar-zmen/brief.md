# Dar Zmen — website brief (verified content only)

Sources: Google Maps business profile (read 2026-09-26: overview, About, Menu tab, photos, the printed-menu photo, reviews) and the lead row in `sahel-website-leads.csv`.

## Facts
- Name: **Dar Zmen**. Google also lists it as "مطعم دار زمان Restaurant dar zmen". The sign over the entrance reads **دار زمان**, with the line **إختصاص أكلة تونسية زمنية** ("speciality: old-time Tunisian food") under it and a **24/24** badge.
- *Dar zmen* is Tunisian Arabic for "house of the old days". The printed menu's own heading for the traditional section is **أطباقنا الزمنية** ("our dishes of the old days"), so the concept comes from the restaurant itself.
- Category: Restaurant, traditional Tunisian cuisine, Monastir.
- Location: plus code **QRFJ+5C Monastir** (35.77299, 10.83103). Google lists no street address. On the map it sits in the old town, near the post office, Monoprix and the fish market, a few minutes' walk from the Ribat. The entrance photo shows a stone gateway in the medina walls.
- Map: https://www.google.com/maps?cid=8740625965181019232
- Phone: **+216 20 181 878** (the number looks like a mobile line; WhatsApp is not confirmed).
- Hours: **open 24/24**, per both Google ("Open 24 hours") and the 24/24 badge on the sign. Still worth confirming with the owner.
- Price: TND 10–20 per person (reported by 46 people).
- Google rating: **4.1 ★ · 174 reviews** (115× 5★, 19× 4★, 9× 3★, 3× 2★, 28× 1★).
- Tripadvisor: #34 of 69 in Monastir (from the lead research; not re-verified).
- Service options: dine-in, takeaway, delivery (Glovo: "Place an order · glovoapp.com"). **Accepts reservations.**
- Google attributes: great coffee; popular for lunch, dinner, solo dining; coffee, halal food, late-night food; catering, dessert, table service; casual, cosy; groups, tourists; good for kids; free street parking, free parking lot, plenty of parking.
- Review keywords shown by Google: tunisian food (18), traditional foods (14), couscous (9), shorba (8).
- Owner's service touches mentioned in reviews and on the Glovo menu: dishes come with bread, salad and soup; free tea.

## Visual identity (from photos)
- Entrance: honey-coloured sandstone gate and crenellated medina wall, arched doorway, and a small dark sign with white Arabic lettering "دار زمان".
- Interior: white walls with painted black line-drawings of old kitchen utensils, a wooden plate rack, hanging copper pots, turquoise wooden chairs, cobalt-blue wrought-iron chairs, a **black-and-white checkerboard floor**, a blue door, a vaulted terracotta-brick ceiling, and a glass counter with the day's dishes on show.
- Tables: blue-and-white Tunisian tile-print cloths, and striped mergoum textiles in red, yellow, blue and green.
- Tableware: Nabeul pottery (white with a cobalt rim, and green/yellow glazed plates).
- Printed menu: white pages framed with **cobalt-blue Tunisian tile patterns**, bold Arabic headings in blue and black, round photo medallions, and a faint watermark of the arched-door logo.
- Palette to design from: sandstone/honey, lime-wash white, cobalt tile blue, turquoise (the chairs), charcoal black (the floor squares), with a warm paprika red from the food.
