01 — THE HOUSE

Dar zmen means the house of the old days.

Behind a stone gateway in the old walls of Monastir, Dar Zmen serves the Tunisian dishes people grew up with: couscous, ojja, mloukhia, kamounia, grilled fish from the coast and chorba. Main dishes come with bread and salad, and the tea is on the house.
