L'Identità

Di più — Italian for more.

A calm, modern trattoria in the centre of Monastir: sage walls, oak slats and rattan lamps inside, a planted green-wall terrace outside. The kitchen covers the Italian classics — wood-blistered pizza, pasta your way (spaghetti, penne, farfalle or tagliatelle), ravioli, risotto — plus grilled meat and fish, mojitos and cheesecakes.
