## Visual identity (from photos)
- Night façade: black frontage, glowing white/blue "Di Più" logo sign, full-height glass.
- Interior: sage-green painted walls and back-bar shelving, vertical oak slat panels, rattan/woven pendant lamps, a wall of woven baskets over a charcoal banquette, leaf-print wallpaper with black-and-white "CIAO" signs, grey stone-look tables, cream bouclé chairs, framed prints.
- Terrace: planted green wall with warm wall-washer lights, black parasols with rattan pendants.
- Printed menu: olive/chartreuse pages and deep grey-green pages, with thin cream high-contrast serif titles ("PLATS", "PASTA", "PIZZA") and a small Italian flag.
- Name meaning: *di più* is Italian for "more". Reviewers mention generous portions, and that is the concept hook.
